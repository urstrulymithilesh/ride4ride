## Problem Statement

Students coordinate carpools, mostly airport runs, in university WhatsApp and Telegram groups. The pattern works, but the container does not travel: posts scroll away unsearchably, post-match coordination has no state, and relocating or visiting means finding and joining a whole new local group and starting over. Ride4Ride is a city and airport scoped classifieds board that carries the pattern across cities. Discovery and connection only. No payments, no routing, no dispatch, no vetting, and permanently no take rate.

## Demand Evidence

- A real university WhatsApp group runs this pattern today, "frequent — posts most weeks," multiple distinct posters. Existing behavior, not stated interest.
- Founder's own calibration, unprompted: scroll-away and post-match coordination "are things I'm confident actually happened and hurt people." Reach is "more of a belief/ambition than something I can point to a concrete missed-ride example for."
- The observable version of reach: per-city group-joining friction. "If you move cities or are just visiting, you have to find and join a whole new local group and repeat the entire process from scratch."
- **Still not collected:** the group is unnamed and uncounted. See The Assignment.

## Status Quo

The competitor is a free group chat that works, not Uber.

> find the right group for your city → join → post or search for a match → chat → coordinate → if it falls through, try again → if nothing works, fall back to a paid cab/rideshare anyway

## Target User & Wedge

A student flying through ORD posts "need ride from ORD airport tomorrow 5pm." Fallback is a cab at roughly $60-100. **About 90% are posted a day or more ahead**, so this is price-shopping against a guaranteed fallback on a deadline, not an emergency. Consequences: asynchronous matching is enough, the churn mechanic is the moment they book the Uber, and arrival vs departure are different shapes sharing one schema.

## Constraints

- Next.js 16.2.10 + React 19.2.4 + Supabase, Vercel. Per `AGENTS.md`, this Next version differs from training data: **read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing framework code.**
- Board, not dispatcher. No payments, no routing, no vetting.
- **Free forever, no take rate.** Permanent. Strongest classification fact; still absent from §10 and the disclaimer (B4).
- **No platform-set price, ever.** See Premise 3.
- **No fake posts or fake accounts on production, ever.** Fixtures are local-dev and test only.
- **Chat privacy is absolute.** Nobody except the two participants can ever read a conversation. Not operators, not analytics, not research. Permanent, no exceptions. See `TODOS.md` P-1.
- **The platform suggests, it never decides.** Post content is always filled in by the user. Defaults are offered and always changeable; nothing is auto-filled on the user's behalf. See `TODOS.md` P-2.
- Mobile-first. Desktop secondary.
- Utility over polish. Mobile data, low-end devices.

## Premises

1. **Open discovery is justified by an observable pain**, per-city group-joining friction. Its cost is the trust container, and that cost is separable if affiliation is *displayed* not *enforced*. **Already built:** `0007` ships `allowed_email_domains` (seeded `.edu`), `is_allowed_student_email()`, and `verify_current_user_email()` setting a `verification_status` badge. It gates nobody. The v2 plan's instruction to retire it was wrong and is withdrawn.
2. **Price is the core value proposition.** "Uber/Lyft already deliver safety, convenience, and reliability as a packaged service. What's missing is affordability."
3. **No platform-set price, ever. Permanent.** Classification turns on *who sets the price*, not on payment processing. A platform-computed cap makes the platform the price-setter, same risk category as a bid field. BlaBlaCar's cap does not transfer: it caps a *driver-set* price under an explicit EU carpooling safe harbor. Money lives only in free text between users, which is user speech. Also ruled out: a poster-typed price field. One clean sentence — the platform has no price field, no exceptions.
4. **Trust and safety depth is deferred, narrowly.** `blocks` and `reports` with RLS ship in `0007`. The pilot trips "strangers matching strangers" on day one, so before launch: 18+ self-attestation, disclaimer shown with acceptance recorded, `reports` reachable everywhere. Deferred: ratings, ride counts, reputation, identity verification, moderation tooling. **Honest scope of the 18+ gate:** with DOB deferred, it is an unverified checkbox plus a disclaimer line. A terms-of-use artifact, not age assurance.

## Design Direction

- **Mobile-first.** Every arriving user taps a link inside a chat thread, on a phone.
- **Clean, modern, minimal, trustworthy.** Restraint is part of the safety argument.
- **Palette: Signal Blue, locked.** Ink `#12181F`, Paper `#F7F8FA`, Signal `#2563EB`. Chosen for highest accent contrast under mobile-first on low-end screens in daylight. Cost accepted: blue-on-cool-white is the most common look on the web, so the product will not be visually distinctive.
- **Three hues only.** Consequence: no dedicated error red. Error and warning states carry via weight, iconography, and copy.

## Login Boundary

**No login:** browsing the feed, viewing an individual post, sharing a post URL.
**Login required:** posting, chatting, contact reveal, reporting, blocking, submitting a wanted-route.

**Already implemented and verified.** `/rides` and `/rides/[id]` use `getUser()` and render regardless; `/rides/new`, `/rides/get`, `/rides/offer`, `/messages`, `/messages/[id]` use `requireUser()`. `lib/auth.ts` documents this exact access model. `CopyLinkButton` already exists on the detail page.

**Still to do:** post detail pages ship `noindex`. Today only `admin/page.tsx` sets `robots: { index: false }`. A public shareable post URL exposing route and travel date should not land in a search engine.

## The Rider / Captain Asymmetry (deliberate, not a gap)

| | Rider post (`type = 'get'`) | Captain post (`type = 'offer'`) |
|---|---|---|
| Address | **Full address required**, geocoded | City/state or airport level only |
| Public display | Masked: street name + city + state, no number | City/state or airport code |
| Distance | Computed and shown | **None, by design** |
| Full address reveal | Mutual-consent handshake (`0005`) | n/a |
| Airport | Derived from geocoded coordinates | Chosen explicitly by the poster |

The DB already encodes this: `0002:70` `constraint rides_distance_only_for_get check (distance_meters is null or type = 'get')`. **The constraint stays.** An earlier eng-review recommendation to drop it was wrong and is withdrawn.

**New work:** the masked street tier does not exist. `rides` has no street column; `ride_locations.from_address` is the full thing. Needs a masked column on `rides` (public side), derived at geocode time, rider posts only.

## Post Expiry

**A post expires exactly 7 days after the ride's scheduled date/time passes**, not 7 days after posting. A ride posted today for three weeks out stays listed until a week after it happens.

- `ride_date IS NOT NULL` → `expires_at = (ride_date + 8 days)` at midnight, i.e. the ride day ends then 7 full days
- `ride_date IS NULL` → `expires_at = created_at + 7 days`

**Correction:** an earlier draft of this section said current/ASAP rides always have `ride_date IS NULL`, "enforced by `rides_future_has_date`". That was written against the `0002` constraint. **`0003` relaxed it** to `check (not is_future or ride_date is not null)`, so a current ride *may* carry a date. The rule above needs no `is_future` branch as a result: if there is a date, it wins.

**Status: SHIPPED in `0008_expiry_hardening.sql` (commit `9bb7dec`).**

Expiry was settable from the client in **four** places, not the three first found:
1. An "Auto-expire after 3/7/14/30 days" `<select>` in the post form.
2. `computeExpiresAt()` in `src/lib/validations/rides.ts`.
3. The `p_expires_at` parameter on both create RPCs.
4. A direct `update rides set expires_at` in `repostRide()`, which the `rides: owner updates own` policy permits.

Fixing only the RPCs would have left (4) open, so the rule is enforced by a **`BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE` trigger** that derives `expires_at` on every write and discards caller input. One rule, one place, closed for code paths that do not exist yet.

Proof: `supabase/tests/expiry_rule.sql`, six assertions including the regression (a far-future ride must outlive its ride date) and that both INSERT and UPDATE discard a supplied expiry.

Watch item, accepted: no ceiling, so a ride booked months out lives months. Stale-post accumulation is monitored rather than prevented (`TODOS.md` T-4).

## Metrics

**Arrival tracking is new and is the gap both the design session and the first review missed.** All three original gates measured conversion and none measured arrival, so a day-21 zero could not distinguish "nobody clicked" from "clicked and bounced" from "no demand" — three results needing opposite responses.

| Metric | Proceed | Ambiguous, extend 2 weeks | Stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Posts by someone other than the founder | ≥ 3 | 1-2 | 0 |
| Conversations started by someone other than the founder | ≥ 5 | 1-4 | 0 |
| Arrivals (unique visits to `/rides` or a post) | ≥ 40 | 10-39 | < 10 |

**Worst row wins.** Any row at zero stops; non-founder posts at zero stops regardless.
**Day-3 checkpoint:** if arrivals are ~0 at day 3, the link is dead (deleted, buried, ignored). Re-share or pick a different group rather than burning 18 more days.
**Founder actions excluded from every row.**

**`wanted_routes` is demoted from a gate to qualitative signal.** It was designed for a board with seeded inventory. With no seeding the board starts empty, so every search misses and every route is "unserved" — it would measure emptiness, clear its bar trivially while posts sat at zero, and then be overridden by worst-row-wins anyway. It is kept because the route *names* tell you where demand clusters, which is the only input for choosing route two.

**Not instrumentable:** "matched before they booked." No signal exists for a rider silently booking an Uber.

**Ruled out permanently, not deferred:** measuring how often money is raised in chat. It required an operator read of private message bodies. Chat privacy is absolute, so this does not return in increment B or anywhere else. Premise 3's cost stays untested by instrumentation, by choice.

## What Ships

1. **Expiry hardening** — compute `expires_at` in both RPCs, drop `p_expires_at`, reconcile `repostRide`.
2. **18+ attestation + acceptance record** — `tos_accepted_at` / `tos_version` on `profiles` (not a separate table). Disclaimer shown, acceptance recorded.
3. **B2 + B4 disclaimer rewrite, before the attestation ships** — zero users pre-launch, so corrected text is version 1 rather than a rewrite forcing re-acceptance on nobody.
4. **`noindex` on post detail.**
5. **Masked street display** for rider posts.
6. **`wanted_routes`** — standing form, arbitrary from/to. Logged-out submit writes server-side with `created_by = null` plus a short-lived cookie holding the row id; claimed after email confirmation. Unclaimed rows expire at 7 days. **Needs rate limiting** — it is an unauthenticated write endpoint and CAPTCHA is off the table.
7. **Airports** — small `airports` table (IATA, lat, lng, radius) + BEFORE INSERT trigger. Rider airport by proximity; captain airport from one new explicit param on `create_offer_ride`. Display as 3-letter IATA.
8. **One-tap quick message** — canned opener, **copy flips by post type** (on a rider post the responder is offering to drive; on a captain post they want a seat). Unthrottled, because the 5-conversation cap is deferred.
9. **Signup counter** — live total, shown from the first user. Cached count revalidated ~60s; not a per-render `count(*)`, not a realtime subscription.
10. **Arrival tracking.**
11. **Tests** — Vitest for server actions and the claim flow; new `supabase/tests/*.sql` for `wanted_routes` RLS and the airport trigger.
12. **IP-suggested FROM city** — the feed's FROM city defaults from the visitor's IP as a *suggestion*, always changeable, any number of times. Never auto-fills a post. Vercel supplies the geo headers, so no third-party service. This was deferred earlier in the review on the grounds that it would strand arrivals on an empty local feed instead of the one seeded route; removing seeding dissolved that objection, since the launch is now organic across groups in several cities.

Single migration where possible; `0008` onward.

## Known Risk: The Feed Renders Empty When the Query Fails

Found 2026-09-02 while verifying local setup. `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` had `/rest/v1/` appended, so every PostgREST call returned `404 PGRST125 "Invalid path specified in request URL"`. The `/rides` page rendered "0 current rides / No current rides posted yet" and returned HTTP 200 with **nothing in the server logs**. A completely broken database was indistinguishable from an empty board.

That is a serious failure mode for this pilot specifically, because the entire day-21 output is "did anyone post." A silent query failure produces the exact reading that triggers the kill condition. The feed must surface a query error as an error state, distinct from the empty state.

**FIXED in `224e471` (T20).** The feed now captures the query error, logs it server-side, and renders a distinct error state that says plainly it is not the same as there being no rides. The count reads "Couldn't load rides" rather than "0". The detail page had the same bug with a worse consequence — a failed query fell through to `notFound()`, telling someone who followed a shared link that the post was deleted — and now throws instead.

Verified by re-injecting the original malformed URL and confirming the two states differ, then restoring the config.

## Known Risk: Two Airport Sources of Truth

Rider airport is derived from coordinates; captain airport is picked by hand. They will disagree at boundaries — a rider geocoded near the ORD/MDW line versus a captain who picked the other one. On a board with three posts that is a silent match failure. Accepted for the pilot, with the mitigation being that the airport is a display and filter aid, not a join key.

## NOT in Scope

- **Seeding of any kind** — founder decision, on honesty grounds. Fixtures stay in local dev.
- **Growth and marketing beyond sharing the link into existing groups** — the platform connects people, it does not manufacture demand or supply. See `TODOS.md` T-1.
- **Route config table** — the feed already supports `?city=&state=&zip=`.
- **Separate `tos_acceptances` table** — two columns on `profiles` cover a pilot-scale fact.
- **`seeded_at` column** — dissolved when seeding was removed.
- **Phone + OTP migration, username, DOB, ratings, ride counts, 5-conversation cap** — increment B, justified by features that need volume that does not exist.
- **Portable cross-city profile** — increment C; depends on B.
- **Price-conversation metric** — needs private-message reads and cannot resolve at pilot scale.
- **Rate limiting / pagination caps on the public feed** — not the wanted-route endpoint, which does need it.

## The Assignment

**Scroll back 60 days in that WhatsApp group and count.** For every real ride post: did it match, how many replies did it get, and was money mentioned — by the poster first, or by the responder?

The group has been described three times across two sessions and is still unnamed and uncounted. The count answers whether "most weeks" is real, what the match rate is, and who raises price first. Twenty minutes, and it is the difference between a memory and a number. It is also the only way to pick which groups to share the link into.
