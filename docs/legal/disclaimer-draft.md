# Legal Disclaimer (Draft)

*This is a starting draft for Ride4Ride, not legal advice. Have an actual lawyer review and finalize this before launch — especially the liability and platform-classification language, which varies by jurisdiction.*

---

## Platform Disclaimer

**Ride4Ride is a peer-to-peer ride-matching platform, not a transportation, rideshare, or taxi service.**

We do not provide rides. We do not employ, contract, dispatch, or supervise drivers ("captains"). We do not process payments for rides. Ride4Ride simply lets users post ride requests and offers and communicate with each other — what happens after that, including whether a ride occurs, is arranged entirely between the users involved.

## No Vetting or Verification

Ride4Ride does **not** conduct background checks, driving record checks, identity verification beyond phone number confirmation, insurance verification, or vehicle inspections on any user. We do not guarantee the identity, driving ability, licensing, insurance coverage, or trustworthiness of any user, whether acting as a rider or a captain.

**You are solely responsible for evaluating who you choose to ride with or transport.** We strongly encourage users to:
- Verify the other person's identity before meeting.
- Share trip details with a friend or family member.
- Meet in public, well-lit locations when possible.
- Confirm vehicle details and license plate before getting in.
- Trust your judgment — if something feels wrong, don't proceed.

## No Liability for Rides

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Ride4Ride, its owners, and operators are not liable for any injury, loss, damage, accident, dispute, delay, theft, or other harm arising from or related to a ride arranged through the platform, whether occurring before, during, or after the ride. This includes disputes between users over cost-sharing arrangements, no-shows, cancellations, vehicle condition, or driving conduct.

## Payments Are Between Users

**Ride4Ride never sets, suggests, calculates, caps, or displays a price for any ride.** There is no price field, no fare estimate, and no suggested cost share anywhere on the platform. If users choose to share costs, they agree the amount themselves, in their own words, between themselves.

**Ride4Ride does not process, hold, guarantee, or facilitate any payment.** Any money exchanged for a ride is a private arrangement between the users involved, made entirely outside the platform, and Ride4Ride bears no responsibility for that exchange.

**Ride4Ride is free and will remain free.** We do not charge users, and we take no fee, commission, or revenue share on any ride, ever.

## Your Conversations Are Private

Messages between two users can be read only by those two users. Ride4Ride does not read conversation contents — not for moderation, not for analytics, not for research, and not to improve the product. There are no exceptions.

If you report a conversation, you choose what to include in that report. Reporting does not grant access to the rest of the thread.

## User Conduct & Reporting

Users are expected to treat each other respectfully and lawfully. Ride4Ride provides reporting and blocking tools so users can flag inappropriate behavior. We may investigate reports and may suspend or remove accounts at our discretion, but we do not guarantee resolution of any dispute between users.

## Age & Eligibility

Users must be **18 years of age or older** to create an account.

## Assumption of Risk

By using Ride4Ride, you acknowledge that arranging and taking rides with people you meet through the platform carries inherent risk, and you voluntarily assume that risk each time you use the service to arrange or take a ride.
